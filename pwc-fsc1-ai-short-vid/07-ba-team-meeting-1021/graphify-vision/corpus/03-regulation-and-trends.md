# Regulation and technology trends shaping AI in insurance

## Taiwan FSC: Guidelines for AI in the Financial Industry (June 2024)
Six core principles: (1) governance and accountability; (2) fairness and human-centric values; (3) privacy and customer rights; (4) system robustness and security; (5) transparency and explainability; (6) sustainable development.
The guideline asks institutions to allow human oversight. "Human-in-the-loop" means humans actively supervise and keep full control.

## Insurance associations: AI self-regulatory rules (FSC approved April 2025, amended 2026)
Insurers remain accountable for AI results, keep an AI inventory, and need internal validation for AI used in underwriting, claims and customer service.

## Gartner Top Strategic Technology Trends 2026 (Oct 2025)
Trends include AI-native development platforms, multiagent systems and domain-specific language models. By 2028 more than half of enterprise generative AI models will be domain-specific. Context is the key differentiator for AI agents.

## McKinsey: Underwriting Operating System (UWOS)
Underwriting moves to "machine-first, human-governed". Work is routed into three lanes (straight-through, assisted decision, expert judgment). Humans move from processing cases to oversight and exception handling. Four steps: lighthouse case, minimum data foundation, define controls, launch targeted workflows.
