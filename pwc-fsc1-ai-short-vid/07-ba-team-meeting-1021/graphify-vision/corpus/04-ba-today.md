# The business analyst today (before)

Typical BA work in a consulting team serving insurers:
- Interview users and write requirement documents, then hand them to the technology team.
- Write meeting minutes and documents manually; quality depends on individual experience.
- Draw static mockups; clients only see the real system after development.
- Business rules and project knowledge live in individual heads; when people change, knowledge restarts from zero.
- Testing happens late; requirements and validation are not linked.
- Scope keeps growing because clients cannot see trade-offs early.
- AI is used ad hoc for summaries, without review standards; clients doubt whether AI output can be trusted.
