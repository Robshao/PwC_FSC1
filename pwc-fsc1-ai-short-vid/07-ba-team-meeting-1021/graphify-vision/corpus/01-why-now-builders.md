# Why now is the time to build (Alexandr Wang, Y Combinator, 2026)

Source: Alexandr Wang in conversation with Garry Tan, "This is a Once-in-a-Civilization Opportunity", Y Combinator, July 2026.

## Abundant intelligence lowers barriers
AI makes intelligence and coding capability cheap and accessible. Small teams and even individual builders ("vibe coders") can now compete with large incumbents. When intelligence and agency become abundant, the new bottleneck is vision and ambition, not raw execution.

## Perennial problems become solvable
Builders can now attack long-standing problems in biology, medicine and foundational science.

## New business and creative models
New product categories, personal AI assistants and business models that were unimaginable are now possible.

## Responsibility to guide risk
The world is not ready for fast-moving AI. Builders have a practical and moral duty to help enterprises and governments adapt, and to solve safety and cybersecurity risks.
