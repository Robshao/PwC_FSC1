# Vision: the business analyst in 2030 (after) — an outside-the-box view

The future BA is a builder-translator who sits between the business, AI agents and engineers.

## Co-creation in the room (forward deployed)
The BA builds working prototypes with AI during the workshop, so clients validate requirements by using them, not by reading documents. Requirements become living prototypes. This depends on hands-on building skill and on business context.

## Orchestrator of AI agent teams
Instead of writing every document, the BA directs a team of AI agents: one drafts requirements, one checks contradictions in meeting notes, one generates test cases. The BA decides, reviews and approves. Orchestration depends on clear acceptance criteria and on structured context.

## Context architect (domain knowledge as a product)
The BA turns insurance rules, terminology, decision logic and exceptions into structured context that AI agents can read. This context library becomes a reusable firm asset that compounds across projects, in line with the trend toward domain-specific models.

## Trust and governance designer
The BA defines what "good enough" means for AI output: acceptance criteria, evals, confidence thresholds, and where a human makes the go / no-go decision. This answers the client's trust question and maps directly to FSC human-in-the-loop and the insurance AI self-regulatory rules.

## Rules-as-code and living requirements
Business rules are written once as executable logic with traceability from requirement to test to production. A change in a rule automatically shows which processes, controls and tests are affected.

## Operating model shaper
The BA redesigns who does what between humans and machines, following the UWOS idea of lanes: straight-through, assisted, expert. The BA designs routing, roles and exception handling.

## Business digital twin
The BA maintains a simulation of the client's process (volumes, rules, routing) so leaders can test a policy change before going live.

## Change and adoption coach
Because users resist new systems, the BA coaches people through the change, turns pain points into adoption plans, and shows value early through prototypes.

## Skills the future BA needs
Hands-on AI prototyping; product prioritization; business architecture; context engineering; eval and acceptance-criteria design; AI governance literacy; storytelling in business language; change leadership.

## How before maps to after
- Gathering requirements and handing off becomes co-creation with working prototypes.
- Manual documents become orchestrated AI agents with human review.
- Knowledge in individual heads becomes a shared context library.
- Late testing becomes rules-as-code with end-to-end traceability.
- Untrusted ad-hoc AI becomes governed AI with explicit acceptance criteria.
- Uncontrolled scope becomes trade-offs tested early in a digital twin.
