# BlackRock: Forward Deployed Business Analyst (public job posting, Oct 2026)

Source: BlackRock careers site, BlackRock Transformation Office (BTO), Associate, Singapore / New York / London.

"This is not a traditional business analysis role centered on gathering requirements and handing them to technology."

The role combines business analysis, product discovery, business architecture and hands-on prototyping to turn ambiguous problems into tangible solutions that business users and engineers can test together.

The ideal profile combines four traits:
- the structured thinking of a business analyst
- the prioritization instincts of a product owner
- the enterprise perspective of a business architect
- the bias to action of a hands-on builder

Responsibilities:
- Translate ambiguous business problems into testable solution hypotheses with measurable outcomes.
- Build structured business context (terminology, entities, relationships, decision logic, data dependencies, controls, exceptions) for use by subject matter experts, AI tools and engineering teams.
- Decompose business processes into explicit logic, controls and exception paths.
- Create functional prototypes using AI-enabled development to discover requirements and validate future-state processes.
- Shape the target operating model: data ownership, governance, controls, roles, exception management.
- Critically evaluate AI-generated outputs, maintain traceability, and understand where human judgment remains essential.
