每段提示詞可以直接整段複製。都要求原始出處、日期、確切數字與網址，避免拿到部落格轉述。查到後把連結或 PDF 給我，我再改進簡報。

## 1. AI 開發成本與生產力：2026 年 vs AI 爆發前（2022 年以前）的比值（給「為什麼要做成產品」頁）

```
I need hard, citable evidence comparing the cost and productivity of software development / AI usage in 2025–2026 versus before the generative-AI boom (2022 or earlier). Please find:

1. The cost of using an AI model of equal capability (e.g., GPT-3.5-level or GPT-4-level performance) per million tokens: the earliest price (around Nov 2022 or Mar 2023) and the latest price (2025 or 2026). Use the Stanford HAI AI Index 2025 and 2026 reports and Epoch AI as primary sources. State the exact ratio (e.g., "280x cheaper") and the page/chart in the report.
2. Controlled studies (RCTs or field experiments) measuring developer productivity with AI coding tools: e.g., GitHub/Microsoft RCT (Peng et al. 2023), the Google internal RCT, the METR 2025 study on experienced open-source developers, Stanford's software engineering productivity research (Yegor Denisov-Blanch), and any 2026 studies. For each: sample size, task type, measured effect (% faster or slower), and whether effects differ for greenfield vs legacy/brownfield code.
3. Any 2026 data on total cost of building a typical enterprise software feature before vs after AI (hours, cost, or headcount).

Rules: Only use primary sources (the original report, paper, or company page). For every number give: publisher, title, publication date, exact figure, the sentence it comes from, and the URL. Flag anything that is only reported by secondary blogs. Separate "AI model price" from "development cost" — they are different things, and do not merge them.
```

## 2. 2026 年 AI 採用：知名大學或頂尖機構的調查（給「保險公司現在就要」頁）

```
Find 2026 (or late-2025 if 2026 is not available) survey data on enterprise AI adoption, preferably in financial services or insurance, from prestigious universities or institutions: Stanford HAI AI Index 2026, MIT (NANDA "The GenAI Divide", MIT Sloan Management Review / BCG), Wharton (Wharton Human-AI Research / GBK "Accountable Acceleration" report), Harvard Business School, Oxford, or Cambridge.

For each, I need:
- The exact statistic (e.g., "88% of organizations use AI in at least one business function")
- Who was surveyed, sample size, country/region, and fieldwork dates
- Whether the figure is the institution's own survey or quoted from another source (e.g., Stanford AI Index quoting McKinsey)

- Any figure about AI governance lagging behind adoption
- Publisher, publication date, page number, and URL

Also verify: "Stanford AI Index 2026 reports 88% organizational AI adoption and 70% using generative AI in at least one function" — is it in the report, on which page, and what is the original source?
Only primary sources. Flag uncertain or secondary-only numbers.
```

## 查證結果：Gemini 回覆的核對（2026-10-29）

| # | Gemini 的說法 | 核對結果 | 簡報怎麼用 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1a | 推理成本降 280 倍 | **正確，但出處是 Stanford AI Index 2025**，不是 2026。GPT-3.5 級（MMLU 約 64.8%）每百萬 token 從 20 美元（2022-11）降到 0.07 美元（2024-10） | 產品化頁照用，標明是「模型使用成本」 |
| 1b | GitHub/Microsoft RCT：效率提升 26%–55% | **把兩篇研究混在一起**。55.8% 是 Peng 等 2023（單一任務：用 JavaScript 寫 HTTP 伺服器，自由接案者）；26.08% 是 Cui 等（Microsoft、Accenture 等三個實地實驗，4,867 位開發者，Management Science 刊出）。另外漏了反證：METR 2025 對 16 位資深開源開發者的實驗，用 AI **反而慢 19%** | 不放「26%–55%」；如要放，兩篇分開寫，並同時講 METR 的反證 |
| 2 | Stanford AI Index 2026：88%，2025 年 71% | **88% 的原始出處是 McKinsey《The state of AI in 2025》**（2025 年 6–7 月，1,993 人、105 國，自填問卷），前一年是 **78%**，不是 71% | 第 5 頁改標 McKinsey，寫「一年前是 78%」 |
| 3 | HBS 758 位「高階」顧問；快 25%、多 12.2%；範圍外低 19 個百分點 | **數字正確**（已讀原文 PDF：多完成 12.2%、快 25.1%、品質高 40% 以上、範圍外正確率低 19 個百分點）。但不是「高階」顧問，是 BCG 的一般顧問（individual contributor），約占該層級 7%。論文後來刊於 Organization Science | 第 10 頁照用 |
| 4 | Veracode 45% | **正確**：80 個寫程式任務、100 多個模型，給模型安全與不安全兩種寫法時，45% 選了不安全的；Java 失敗率 72% | 照用「45% 的任務選了不安全的寫法」，不要說成「45% 的程式有漏洞」 |
| 5 | JPMorgan、Goldman Sachs 也在招前線部署 BA | **沒有附任何來源，無法核實**。目前只核實 BlackRock | 只引 BlackRock |
| 6 | 人工迴歸測試占 QA 週期 60–70%，自動化縮短到 1/5 以下 | **沒有出處，像廠商行銷數字** | 不用 |
